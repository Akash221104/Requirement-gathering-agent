An AI-powered Requirements Gathering Assistant that works alongside customers and requirement engineers during meetings to automate and intelligently manage the software requirement engineering lifecycle. It captures and understands meeting conversations, extracts functional and non-functional requirements, identifies missing, ambiguous, or conflicting information, and generates relevant clarification questions. It automatically creates structured requirement documents (SRS), maintains context across multiple meetings, tracks evolving requirements, and performs change-impact analysis to identify affected modules and dependencies when requirements change. The assistant can actively participate in discussions with customers and engineers, provide intelligent suggestions, and update documentation. A human requirement engineer remains responsible for reviewing, validating, and approving all final requirements, ensuring accuracy and human control.
